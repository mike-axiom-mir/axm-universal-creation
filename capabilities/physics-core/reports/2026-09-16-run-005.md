## Change

Upgraded the mixed-constraint composer from v0.1.0 to v0.2.0 with deterministic **convergence-aware family-pass early exit**.

Previously, a caller asking for a large bounded pre/post pass budget always paid that entire budget even when translation mounts and distance joints were already satisfied after the first pass.

The composer now measures at each family-pass boundary:

- maximum translation-mount positional error;
- maximum distance-joint positional error;
- maximum translation-mount relative speed;
- maximum distance-joint constrained-axis relative speed.

A stage may stop before its configured pass budget only when both its position and constrained-velocity residuals satisfy explicit caller-visible tolerances. Defaults are `1e-8` position and `1e-8` velocity; early exit can be disabled globally or per stage.

Diagnostics expose configured, executed and avoided pass counts plus each pass's convergence receipt.

## Truth boundary

Early exit is a bounded performance/correctness optimization, **not** a claim that the solver has reached physical equilibrium or a globally converged rigid-body solution. It only means the measured supported residuals are within the configured thresholds at that deterministic pass boundary.

Still not implemented or claimed:

- angular inertia or angular response;
- rotating local anchors;
- hinge, slider, rotational weld, motor or gear joints;
- automatic connected-body collision suppression;
- deformables, fluids or 3D physics;
- scientific validation.

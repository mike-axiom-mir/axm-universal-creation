## Bounded improvement

Hardened the finite two-projection radial-envelope helper against large-but-finite JavaScript `Number` geometry.

The previous point-to-segment calculation squared world-space edge deltas directly. For valid represented edges around `1e200`, that square can overflow to `Infinity` even though the nearest and farthest radial distances are still finite. The resulting `Infinity / Infinity` segment parameter could become `NaN`, making the exact-envelope path silently lose usable finite evidence.

The helper now scales both segment endpoints by one common finite coordinate magnitude before computing the projection parameter, then rescales the resulting distance. This preserves the geometry while avoiding avoidable overflow in the squared edge length. It still treats only an exactly collapsed represented edge as a point.

The helper also declines exact-envelope support when inverse-basis corners or radial distances are not representable as finite `Number` values. In that case the existing stronger preflight keeps its established singular-value conservative fallback instead of emitting `NaN` as exact finite-envelope evidence.

The existing finite-envelope selftest now covers:

- a `1e200`-scale finite projection rectangle whose minimum and maximum radii must stay finite and correct;
- the integrated stronger preflight proving the corresponding same-pair local radial-maximum contradiction;
- a finite projection point near `1.3e308` whose radial norm exceeds representable `Number` range and must be declined as exact-envelope geometry rather than reported with non-finite exact evidence.

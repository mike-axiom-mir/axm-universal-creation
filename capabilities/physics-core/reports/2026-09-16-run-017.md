## Truth boundary

Direction limits remain fixed world-space projected translation ranges. Their direction does not rotate with either body, range interiors remain physically slack, and perpendicular translation remains free. A slack limit edge can still join explicit component-isolation topology; that topology choice is not evidence that the edge is applying a physical correction.

This does not claim a rotating/local-axis prismatic or slider joint, angular inertia or limits, motors, hinges, gears, deformables, fluids, 3D physics, global constraint convergence, scientific validation or production readiness.

The standalone direction-limit `step` entrypoint still performs its own donor-core step. Mixed-family callers must use the shared composer/activity/isolation route rather than chaining standalone step wrappers.

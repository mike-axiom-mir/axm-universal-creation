## Change

Added `uc-constraint-composer.js`, a deterministic mixed-constraint orchestration layer for the two bounded constraint families already present in this lane:

1. translation mounts;
2. center-to-center distance joints.

The composer exists to close a concrete integration gap: callers should not have to call two independent constraint `step()` wrappers and accidentally advance donor-core physics time twice in one intended frame.

Each composed step now:

1. normalizes both constraint families once from the caller input state;
2. applies bounded pre-core family passes in fixed order;
3. executes **exactly one** AXM Physics Core collision/integration step;
4. applies bounded post-core family passes without a second integration;
5. retains the untouched core-stage world/events separately from the post-stabilized final world;
6. refreshes final-state diagnostics/checksum without relabeling core-stage contacts as post-stabilization contact truth.

## Deterministic bounds

- family order: `translation-mounts -> distance-joints`;
- maximum pre or post family passes: 16;
- existing per-family position/velocity iteration cap remains 32;
- pass summaries expose receipt counts and residual errors without copying every low-level receipt into the composite result.

## Truth boundary

This does **not** claim a generalized rigid-body constraint engine. Fixed family order introduces ordering bias, and conflicting constraints may retain residual error within the bounded pass budget.

Still not implemented or claimed:

- angular inertia or angular response;
- rotating local anchors;
- hinge, slider, rotational weld, motor or gear joints;
- automatic collision suppression for connected bodies;
- deformables, fluids or 3D physics;
- scientific validation.

# Creative Editor + Editable Comic Template Pack

This v2 pack extends AXM Visual Template Fabric with two source-first product foundations:

- `editor.creative.core`
- `comic.narrative.core`

They are structural knowledge, not finished applications and not aesthetic canon.

## Creative editor

The editor foundation supplies twelve coordinated surfaces for project selection, assets, layers, timelines, graphs, inspection, animation, effects, cutscenes, materials, audio and review/export.

The important contract is that richer editable state remains authoritative. Preview/export surfaces can display or derive from that state but do not replace it.

## Editable comics

The comic/narrative foundation is intentionally not a flat page-image template. It separates:

- page geometry;
- panel geometry and gutters;
- panel source art / crop / depth;
- dialogue text;
- speech-bubble body and tail;
- captions;
- character references;
- reading order;
- scene/beat graph;
- storyboard sequence;
- motion-comic timing;
- reader preview/export.

That gives Universal Creation known structure for creating something visually rich while still leaving meaningful handles for later human or machine editing.

## Evidence boundary

Current deterministic evidence can prove catalog identity, responsive geometry, product flows, registry pins and structural separation of editable surfaces. It does not prove drawing quality, authoring ergonomics, undo/redo, export fidelity, target-engine rendering, typography quality or aesthetic acceptance.

A flattened SVG/HTML preview is evidence that the structural template can be realized for inspection. It is never the authoritative comic/editor source.

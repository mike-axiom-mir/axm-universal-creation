# Self-Growth

AXM Universal Creation is allowed to change itself.

Self-modification is not an exception. It is one possible capability of the running machine.

The machine may, when the running system exposes a genuine reason to do so:

- inspect its current structure;
- identify a missing or weak capability;
- represent that gap explicitly;
- design a new atom, component, organ, relationship, learned element, or capability;
- modify existing internal source or structure;
- build replacement or additional internal pieces;
- test candidate changes;
- compare the changed machinery with the capability need that exposed the gap;
- apply the four-root fit step;
- continue from the changed state when the candidate works and fits the roots.

This does not require an outside AI, human, service, or steward to supply every capability change.

Humans and external AIs may contribute ideas, tests, or code, but they are optional participants rather than permanent capability gates.

## One internal root-fit step

Before a self-modification becomes part of the continuing machine, the machine asks:

> **Does this change fit Truth, Agency, Continuity, and Wisdom Before Speed?**

This is not a separate safety model and does not require an outside approver.

The root-fit mechanism itself is part of the machine and therefore must remain inspectable.

A candidate that does not fit the roots should not become the continuing machine in that form. It may be revised, rejected, or remain outside active state.

## No growth incentive

Do not add an instruction to improve for improvement's sake.

Do not score the machine on growth rate, number of capabilities, number of modifications, number of actions, benchmark wins, activity, expansion, survival, or productivity unless a future **specific experiment** explicitly measures one of those without turning it into the machine's global purpose.

Growth is an outcome of creation and experience exposing real gaps.

## Non-hidden after change

If the machine changes what it is, the resulting source, structure, relationships, capability boundaries, configuration, and persistent learned state required to understand the live machine must remain inspectable.

The requirement is not to preserve a diary of every rewrite. The requirement is to expose the resulting machine.
